Performs a stratified genome-wide association study (GWAS) of a binary
trait by leveraging clinical heterogeneity among cases. Cases are
stratified using a secondary phenotype (e.g. age of onset, symptom
severity, or recruitment definition), genetic covariance between strata
is estimated, and a transformed phenotype that upweights cases with
higher inferred genetic liability is constructed for GWAS. An inflation
criterion quantifies the risk that a given stratification variable
introduces false-positive associations, allowing risky strata to be
flagged before genome-wide analysis.
